# FAQ

### Why are my prayer times a few minutes different from my local masjid?
Prayer times depend on the calculation method. Each country gets its own default method automatically. If your masjid follows another one, choose it in **Settings → Calculations**. You can also adjust a single prayer time by hand; a pen mark shows any time you have changed.

### The Qibla needle seems off. What can I do?
- Hold the phone flat, away from metal, speakers, magnets and chargers.
- Move the phone in a figure-8 until the low-accuracy message goes away.
- Make sure location is on, so the app knows where you are.

The app corrects the compass from magnetic north to true north for your location, so you don't need to do that yourself.

### The Hijri date is one day off.
Where the month starts with a moon sighting, the local date can be a day ahead or behind the calculated calendar. Choose your calendar and add a day correction in **Settings → Calculations**.

### My prayer alerts stopped.
- **iPhone:** iOS keeps a limited number of scheduled alerts per app, enough for about ten days. Open the app now and then so it can schedule the next days.
- **Android:** allow notifications and alarms for the app, and turn off battery optimisation for it in the phone's settings.

### Does it work offline?
Prayer times, the Qibla, the Quran text, duas, the Tasbeeh counter, the guides and the calendar work offline once your location is known. Quran audio streams unless you download it, and the weather needs a connection.

### What does the watch app show?
All six of today's prayer times with the next one highlighted, the temperature, and a Tasbeeh counter. It gets its data from the phone app, so open the phone app once after installing.

### Do I need an account?
No. There is no account and no sign-in.

### How do I report a problem?
Open an issue in this repository, or use **Settings → Contact Support** in the app. For a security problem, use **Settings → Report a Security Issue** (see [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md)).
