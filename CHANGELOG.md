# Changelog

One entry per store release, from the What's New notes. The newest is first.

## 4.0.2 — November 2026
- App Store: 26 November 2026. Google Play: in review.
- iPad: Calendar, Library and Settings now share Home's layout, with titles at the same height and cards in the same column.
- Hisn al-Muslim: a dua link or a Daily Dua widget tap now opens right at that dua, even the first time.
- Selected Duas: long Arabic duas no longer run under the favourite heart.
- General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## 4.0.1 — November 2026
- App Store: 24 November 2026. Google Play: replaced by 4.0.2 before it was published.
- A fresh Home: your next prayer on a panel at the top, then the Qibla, the six prayer times and the Counter.
- Weather and Counter refreshed with new backgrounds.
- Daily Dua widget: now in medium size too; it changes at Fajr and at Maghrib, and a tap opens that dua.
- Small widget redesigned, with the temperature in the corner.
- General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## 4.0.0 — November 2026
- App Store: 14 November 2026. Google Play: 18 November 2026.
- A whole new look in deep blue and gold, and a new app icon.
- A new Home: your next prayer with a live countdown, all six times in one card.
- Weather redesigned, with the Hijri date.
- Mushaf view: the Quran page by page, like the printed Madani Mushaf, in the King Fahd Complex font.
- Two pages side by side on foldables and tablets.
- Daily Dua widget: a new dua on your Home Screen each day.
- Selected Duas: 92 short duas in 13 themes, each with its source; save your favourites.
- Manasik: the Talbiyah and the duas of Tawaf and Sa'i, with a pronunciation guide in Latin letters.
- More national calculation methods and more cities.
