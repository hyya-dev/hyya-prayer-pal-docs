# Changelog

One entry per store release, from the What's New notes. The newest is first.

## 3.7.3 — November 2026
- App Store: 9 November 2026.
- General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## 3.7.2 — November 2026
- Settings: a Report a Security Issue row and a Contact Support row; the rating and hYYa cards can be tapped anywhere.
- Widgets: the temperature sits below the mascot on the medium widget and is centred on the large one.
- General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## 3.7.1 — November 2026
- The Qibla compass now corrects for the difference between magnetic north and true north at your location, and asks for a figure-8 calibration when needed.

## 3.7.0 — November 2026
- New widgets in small, medium and large, designed for light and dark mode.
- Prayer times follow each country's own calculation method by default.

## 3.6.2 — October 2026
- Easier to use with screen readers and voice control, with clearer, easier-to-read text.

## 3.6.1 — October 2026
- Made for iPhone Duo, with two pages side by side when it's open.

## 3.6.0 — October 2026
- A rating card at the top of Settings, and links that open a screen of the app directly.

## 3.5.8 — October 2026
- New app, watch and widget icons.

## 3.5.7 — October 2026
- An age check at first launch, as some app-store age laws require.

## 3.5.5 — September 2026
- More reliable location when moving between cities.

## 3.5.4 — September 2026
- Islamic calendar fixes, a weak-GPS indicator, and corrected Quran translations.

## 3.5.1 — September 2026
- One Hijri calendar across the app, set in Settings.

## 3.5.0 — August 2026
- The Islamic Calendar page, with occasions you can add to your phone's calendar.
